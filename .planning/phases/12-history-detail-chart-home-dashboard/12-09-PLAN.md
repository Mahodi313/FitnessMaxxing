<objective>
Close FIT-109 (major): finishing a session does not refresh the Home activity ring / sessions-this-week count — it only updates when the next session starts.

Root cause (confirmed in code): `app/lib/query/client.ts` line 778 registers the `['session','finish']` mutation defaults. Its `onSettled` (L829-842) invalidates `sessionsKeys.active()`, `sessionsKeys.detail(id)`, `lastValueKeys.all`, and `sessionsKeys.listInfinite()` — but NOT `dashboardKeys`. The `get_dashboard_summary` aggregate (sessions-this-week, streak, weekly volume, sparkline series, lifetime stats) therefore keeps its stale cache until an unrelated refetch trigger fires. Starting a new session re-mounts/refocuses surfaces that happen to refetch, which is why the count "catches up" only on the second session.

Purpose: The Home hero (DASH-01/02) and the History volume card + lifetime eyebrow (DASH-03/04, D-05 split) both read the single `dashboardKeys.summary()` slot (D-03). Invalidating it on finish makes the ring/count/volume reflect the just-finished session immediately.

Output: One added `invalidateQueries({ queryKey: dashboardKeys.all })` call inside the existing `['session','finish']` `onSettled`, plus the `dashboardKeys` import wired into client.ts.
</objective>
